LPJ-EOSIM is a dynamic global vegetation model that helps us understand how the land carbon cycle responds to a changing climate.

Climate inputs

It ingests climate variables, like precipitation, air temperature, incoming radiation, and atmospheric CO₂.

Simulated quantities

Using those inputs, it simulates a dynamic world where plants grow, compete, and die from first principles in a coupled soil, water, and biosphere continuum. It models key ecosystem variables, from soil carbon to N₂O emissions.

Parameterizations of the Earth system

To do this efficiently, it uses parameterizations of key processes, like photosynthesis, fire disturbance, and the flow of carbon and nitrogen through the soil.

Changes over time

Using this model we can try to understand how large-scale processes have shaped our world and will continue to shape it. The impacts of changing land use, increasing CO₂, and warming temperatures are all simulated in the same unified framework.
